# Grooming and Coat Care

## Brushing
Brushing frequency depends on coat type. Short-haired pets do well with a
weekly brushing, while long-haired or double-coated pets often need brushing
several times a week or daily to prevent mats. Regular brushing removes loose
hair, spreads natural oils, and is a good time to check the skin for problems.

## Bathing
Most dogs only need a bath every one to three months, or when they are dirty.
Bathing too often can dry out the skin. Use a shampoo made for pets, because
human shampoo has the wrong pH. Cats groom themselves and rarely need baths.

## Nail trimming
Trim nails every three to four weeks, or when you hear them clicking on the
floor. Trim only the tip to avoid cutting the quick, the pink area inside the
nail that contains blood vessels. If you cut too far and it bleeds, apply
styptic powder and keep the pet calm.

## Ears, teeth, and eyes
Check ears weekly for redness, odor, or discharge and wipe the outer ear gently
if dirty. Brush teeth several times a week with pet toothpaste to prevent dental
disease. Wipe away eye discharge with a damp cloth. Persistent discharge, odor,
or redness should be checked by a vet.
