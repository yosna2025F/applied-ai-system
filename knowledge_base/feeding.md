# Feeding and Nutrition

## How often to feed
Adult dogs are usually fed twice a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart. Puppies
under six months need three to four smaller meals a day because they cannot
store enough energy between large meals. Adult cats do well with two measured
meals a day, though many cats prefer several small portions.

## Portion sizes
Portion size depends on weight, age, and activity level. Follow the feeding
chart printed on the food packaging as a starting point, then adjust so the
pet keeps a healthy body condition. You should be able to feel the ribs
without pressing hard, and the waist should be visible from above.

## Fresh water
Always keep clean, fresh water available. Refill the bowl at least once a day
and wash it regularly. A pet that suddenly drinks much more or much less water
than usual may need a vet visit.

## Foods that are unsafe
Chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol (a sweetener), and alcohol
are toxic to dogs and cats. Keep these away from pets at all times. Cooked
bones can splinter and should not be given.

## Transitioning foods
When switching to a new food, mix it with the old food and increase the new
portion gradually over 7 days. A sudden change often causes an upset stomach.
