# Exercise and Walking

## Daily exercise needs
Most dogs need at least 30 to 60 minutes of exercise a day, but this varies by
breed and age. High-energy working breeds may need two hours or more, while
older or short-nosed breeds need less. A bored dog with too little exercise
often develops chewing, barking, or digging problems.

## Walking guidance
Walk dogs on a well-fitted harness or collar with a leash in public areas. In
hot weather, walk early in the morning or later in the evening to avoid heat
and hot pavement. Press the back of your hand to the pavement for seven
seconds; if it is too hot for your hand, it is too hot for paws.

## Cats and indoor play
Indoor cats need daily active play to stay healthy and avoid boredom. Short
sessions of 10 to 15 minutes with a wand toy or chase toy, a few times a day,
work well. Climbing shelves and scratching posts also provide exercise.

## Signs of overexertion
Stop exercising and rest the pet if you see heavy panting that does not slow
down, stumbling, or unwillingness to continue. Provide water and shade. Repeated
overexertion in heat can lead to heatstroke, which is an emergency.
