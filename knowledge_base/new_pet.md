# Bringing Home a New Pet

## Prepare before arrival
Before a new pet arrives, set up a quiet space with a bed, water, food, and a
few toys. Remove hazards such as loose wires, toxic plants, and small objects
that could be swallowed. Having supplies ready lets the pet settle without
extra stress.

## The first days
New pets need time to adjust. Keep the first days calm and predictable, with a
consistent feeding and potty routine. Let the pet explore one room at a time
rather than the whole home at once. It is normal for a new pet to hide, eat
less, or be quiet for the first day or two.

## Routine and house-training
A steady daily routine helps pets learn faster. Take puppies out to the same
spot after meals, naps, and play, and reward success right away. Consistency and
patience work far better than punishment, which increases fear and slows
learning.

## Introductions to other pets
Introduce a new pet to existing pets slowly, over several days. Start by letting
them smell each other's bedding, then allow short, supervised meetings, and
increase the time as they stay relaxed. Never force interactions.

## First vet visit
Schedule a first wellness visit within the first week. The vet can confirm the
pet is healthy, start or continue vaccinations, and set up parasite prevention.
