# Vaccination and Preventive Care

## Puppy and kitten schedule
Puppies and kittens usually start core vaccinations at 6 to 8 weeks of age,
with boosters every 3 to 4 weeks until about 16 weeks old. This series builds
protection as the immunity from their mother fades. Your veterinarian sets the
exact schedule based on the pet and local requirements.

## Core vaccines
Core vaccines for dogs typically include distemper, parvovirus, adenovirus, and
rabies. Core vaccines for cats typically include panleukopenia, calicivirus,
herpesvirus, and rabies. Rabies vaccination is required by law in many places.

## Adult boosters
After the initial series, adult pets receive boosters on a schedule set by the
vet, commonly every one to three years depending on the vaccine. Keeping a
written record of dates makes it easy to know when the next booster is due.

## Parasite prevention
Year-round flea, tick, and heartworm prevention is recommended in most regions.
Heartworm is prevented with monthly medication and is far easier to prevent
than to treat. Ask a vet which products fit your pet's weight and species.

## Routine checkups
A yearly wellness checkup helps catch problems early. Senior pets, usually
those over seven years old, often benefit from checkups twice a year.
